# Indian Superstore Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project analyzes Indian Superstore sales data using PostgreSQL and Microsoft Excel. It focuses on sales, profit, quantity, products, regions, outlet types, and shipping modes.

## Objective
To analyze Indian Superstore Sales data and identify trends and patterns in sales, profit, quantity, products, regions, outlet types and shipping modes. SQL was used for data analysis and Excel was used to create PivotTables, PivotCharts, interactive slicers and a dynamic dashboard for business decision-making.

## Dataset Overview
- Total Orders: 100,000
- Total Quantity: 5,49,376
- Total Sales: ₹250.84 crore
- Total Profit: ₹37.55 crore
- Profit Margin: 14.97%
- Years: 2019–2023
- Regions: East, North, South, West
- Outlet Types: Large, Medium, Small
- Ship Modes: First Class, Same Day, Second Class, Standard Class

## Key Business Insights
- Total sales were approximately ₹250.84 crore with total profit of approximately ₹37.55 crore.
- Sales remained relatively stable from 2019 to 2021, followed by a decline during 2022 and 2023.
- The East region recorded the highest sales and profit among the regions analyzed.
- Medium and Large outlet types generated similar sales, while Medium outlets recorded the highest profit.
- Quantity and profit were relatively balanced across shipping modes.
- Several products appeared among the top products by sales or profit.

## Business Recommendations
1. Focus on high-performing products.
2. Investigate the sales decline after 2021.
3. Study strong regional performance and useful practices for other regions.
4. Optimize outlet performance using both sales and profit.
5. Monitor shipping performance using delivery time, cost and customer satisfaction in addition to sales and profit.
6. Use the dashboard for ongoing business monitoring and decision-making.
